# Newsletter preferences

<!-- intents: newsletter_subscription -->

## Subscribe

Turn on the newsletter from account preferences and confirm the registration email within 24 hours.

General policy applies across the fictional KharidiKart store. Check My Orders for details tied to a particular purchase. The demo displays order information only after selecting the account that owns the order.

Use the account that owns the email address when enabling the subscription. Confirm the message only if you initiated the request, and use the newest confirmation if you asked for a resend. Your preference can be changed later in account settings.

## Unsubscribe

Use the unsubscribe link or disable newsletters in account preferences. The change takes up to 2 working days.

Use the account portal for a self-service change when the option is available. When contacting support about an existing purchase, keep the order reference handy and describe the outcome you need in a short message.

Open the newsletter preference page to check whether marketing messages are currently enabled. A message already queued may arrive while the setting is being applied. If newsletters keep arriving after the processing window, contact support and mention when you changed the preference.

## Order updates

Order and refund notices continue when marketing email is disabled. Each subscription applies to one email address.


Transactional notices cover activity such as payment confirmation, dispatch or a refund. Keep your account email current so you can receive these updates separately from marketing. If you have several accounts, change or confirm the subscription for the email address receiving the unwanted messages.
