# Delivery periods

<!-- intents: delivery_period -->

## Metro

Standard shipments to eligible metro pin codes typically arrive in 2–4 working days.

General policy applies across the fictional KharidiKart store. Check My Orders for details tied to a particular purchase. The demo displays order information only after selecting the account that owns the order.

The working-day estimates apply to eligible service areas and start after dispatch. Check the destination pin code for the promise shown at checkout. Once you receive an order reference, open My Orders to see the estimate tied to that shipment. If a carrier sends a revised date, use the new estimate when planning to receive the parcel.

## Other cities

Standard shipments outside metro areas typically arrive in 4–7 working days. Check the order for its own estimate.

Use the account portal for a self-service change when the option is available. When contacting support about an existing purchase, keep the order reference handy and describe the outcome you need in a short message.

Delivery times for towns outside metro coverage vary with carrier schedules and the shipping service shown at checkout. A rural delivery address may need extra carrier coordination. Keep the address details and pin code accurate. If the estimate passes and the parcel remains in transit, raise a ticket using your order reference.

## Express window

Eligible express shipments typically arrive in 1–2 working days after dispatch.


For express orders, use the live tracking status to tell whether the package has actually left fulfilment. Time spent waiting before dispatch is separate from the delivery estimate. Weekends and holidays can affect working-day calculations. Contact support if a parcel is marked delivered and you cannot locate it.
