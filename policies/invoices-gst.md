# Invoices and GST

<!-- intents:  -->

## Availability

An invoice belongs to one order and is available to the signed-in owner. Search using your order ID.

General policy applies across the fictional KharidiKart store. Check My Orders for details tied to a particular purchase. The demo displays order information only after selecting the account that owns the order.

After opening My Orders, select the purchase you want and use its invoice option. Verify that the order reference matches the shipment before saving or sharing the document. If your purchase contains items ordered at different times, download each document using its own reference. Support can assist when the invoice option is missing.

## Amounts

The order invoice shows its GST and total in INR. Amounts shown on the invoice take precedence over catalogue estimates.

Use the account portal for a self-service change when the option is available. When contacting support about an existing purchase, keep the order reference handy and describe the outcome you need in a short message.

The tax and total printed on the issued invoice relate to that purchase. Comparing the displayed total with a payment record can help identify which charge to investigate. When raising an issue, include the invoice reference and identify the disputed line so support can locate it quickly.

## Business details

Ask within 2 working days of purchase if your business billing details are missing. Have the order reference ready.


For a business billing request, gather the invoice reference and the business details displayed at checkout. Submit the request from the account that owns the purchase. Avoid including unrelated financial information in the message. Keep a copy of the request and the invoice while the review is pending.
