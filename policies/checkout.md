# Order placement

<!-- intents: place_order -->

## Place an order

Add items to the cart, check delivery details, and confirm payment from checkout. Each checkout creates one order reference.

General policy applies across the fictional KharidiKart store. Check My Orders for details tied to a particular purchase. The demo displays order information only after selecting the account that owns the order.

Review quantities, delivery details and the total before confirming payment. The checkout page shows available services and payment options for the destination. After completion, save the confirmation or open My Orders to check that the reference belongs to the intended purchase.

## Reservations

A cart does not reserve stock. If checkout offers a reservation, it lasts up to 10 minutes.

Use the account portal for a self-service change when the option is available. When contacting support about an existing purchase, keep the order reference handy and describe the outcome you need in a short message.

Stock and delivery availability can change between adding an item and completing checkout. If a reservation expires, review the cart before trying again. Check the amount and delivery estimate after changing a quantity, applying an offer or selecting another address.

## Confirmation email

The confirmation email usually arrives within 15 minutes. You can also look up purchases in My Orders.


Check spam and any inbox filters if the order confirmation email does not appear. Use the order page for the current status if payment has already been processed. If you cannot see a reference and the payment record shows a debit, follow the payment problems policy before attempting to pay again.
