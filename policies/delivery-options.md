# Delivery options

<!-- intents: delivery_options -->

## Standard

Standard shipping costs ₹49 for eligible orders below ₹999 and is free on eligible orders of ₹999 or more.

General policy applies across the fictional KharidiKart store. Check My Orders for details tied to a particular purchase. The demo displays order information only after selecting the account that owns the order.

Use the price and service displayed for the delivery address at checkout. The order subtotal and pin code determine which options appear. If you change the cart or destination, review the updated fee again before confirming payment. Orders placed separately each receive an independent shipping quote.

## Express

Express shipping costs ₹99 in eligible pin codes. Availability appears at checkout.

Use the account portal for a self-service change when the option is available. When contacting support about an existing purchase, keep the order reference handy and describe the outcome you need in a short message.

Express handling depends on the package contents and the destination. If your cart holds items that are unavailable for express service, checkout may offer standard shipping. For a time-sensitive gift or replacement, confirm the delivery estimate before placing the order. Once an order exists, My Orders shows the service and estimated date associated with that reference.

## Pickup

Store pickup is free where it is offered. Hold time after the ready-for-pickup notice is 3 calendar days.


Wait for the ready-for-pickup message before visiting the store. Bring the order confirmation and follow the pickup instructions shown in the account. Leave enough time to collect before the hold period ends. If the pickup location is unavailable or the hold period has expired, contact support with the order reference.
