# Payment problems

<!-- intents: payment_issue -->

## Pending payment

Wait 30 minutes before retrying a pending payment. Check My Orders so you can avoid placing a duplicate order.

General policy applies across the fictional KharidiKart store. Check My Orders for details tied to a particular purchase. The demo displays order information only after selecting the account that owns the order.

Check My Orders and the payment app for the most recent status before attempting another payment. If the order is visible and active, use its reference for any follow-up. A bank may display a pending authorization while the retailer is waiting for confirmation.

## Debited without order

If payment was debited and no order appears after 24 hours, contact support with the payment reference.

Use the account portal for a self-service change when the option is available. When contacting support about an existing purchase, keep the order reference handy and describe the outcome you need in a short message.

If no order appears after the stated window, gather the payment reference, amount, and approximate time of the attempted checkout. Open a ticket from the signed-in help area so the support team can reconcile the record. Keep banking credentials out of the message and use the same ticket for updates.

## Duplicate charge

Report a duplicate payment within 7 calendar days and provide both payment references. Keep PINs and verification codes private.


If you notice two entries, compare their references with the purchase, including whether one entry is still pending. A duplicate-charge report should identify both transactions. Retain the statement entries until the review is finished, and track any resulting refund from the account that placed the order.
