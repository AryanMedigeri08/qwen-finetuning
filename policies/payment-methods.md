# Payment methods

<!-- intents: check_payment_methods -->

## Online methods

Checkout accepts UPI, supported credit or debit cards, and net banking. Choose one payment method per order.

General policy applies across the fictional KharidiKart store. Check My Orders for details tied to a particular purchase. The demo displays order information only after selecting the account that owns the order.

Use the checkout screen to see which payment options are available for the current order and address. If a method fails, check the orders page and your transaction history before starting another payment. Save the payment reference for support if funds were debited while the order remains missing.

## Cash on delivery

Cash on delivery is available for eligible orders of up to ₹5,000 and appears only for supported pin codes.

Use the account portal for a self-service change when the option is available. When contacting support about an existing purchase, keep the order reference handy and describe the outcome you need in a short message.

For cash on delivery, keep the amount shown on the order confirmation ready. Check any delivery message against the order details before acting on it. If COD disappears after editing an address or cart, review the revised checkout options for that purchase. A completed COD purchase can have a separate registered refund process after cancellation or return.

## Payment information

KharidiKart support never asks for your card PIN, OTP, or UPI PIN. Complete payment only through checkout.


If someone asks for an OTP or PIN while claiming to represent support, close the message and open KharidiKart from the account portal. Describe the request through a fresh support ticket. Share the transaction reference when appropriate and leave payment credentials out of the description.
