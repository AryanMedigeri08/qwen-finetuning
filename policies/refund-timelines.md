# Refund timelines

<!-- intents: check_refund_policy -->

## UPI

UPI refunds typically take 3–5 working days from initiation, with an estimated upper limit of 5 working days.

General policy applies across the fictional KharidiKart store. Check My Orders for details tied to a particular purchase. The demo displays order information only after selecting the account that owns the order.

Look for the original UPI transaction in the payment app or bank statement linked to the order. Allow for weekends and holidays when comparing the elapsed time to working days. If the expected period has passed, open the refund record and have its identifier ready before contacting support. The order status alone gives no information about the payment provider's progress.

## Card and net banking

Card and net banking refunds typically take 5–7 working days from initiation.

Use the account portal for a self-service change when the option is available. When contacting support about an existing purchase, keep the order reference handy and describe the outcome you need in a short message.

For a card payment, check the card statement used for the purchase. Depending on the bank, a posted refund can appear as a credit or as a reversal of the original transaction. With net banking, check the account used for payment. If you no longer have access to that account or card, discuss the next steps using a support ticket tied to the purchase.

## Cash on delivery

Cash-on-delivery refunds may take up to 10 working days from initiation. Support can help with the refund destination.


For a cash-on-delivery refund, wait for the refund record to be created before checking its estimate. Use only support channels reached through your account. Keep the order ID and refund reference and share them when asking the team to investigate a delay.
