# Refund policy

<!-- intents: check_refund_policy -->

## Eligibility

Request a refund for an eligible delivered order within 7 calendar days of delivery. Include the order number and a short reason.

Keep the reference until the case is resolved. If a refund already exists, check that record before requesting further assistance. An order status and a refund status describe different stages of the purchase.

The eligibility window starts on the delivery date shown for the item. Choose the affected purchase from My Orders and describe what happened, such as a damaged item or an incorrect product. The order must belong to the account making the request. For questions about stock-dependent replacements, check the returns and exchanges policy before choosing a refund.

## Confirmation

Refund initiation requires explicit confirmation for the specified order. Each order has at most one refund record.

If the method or timing is unclear, check the refund timeline policy for the original payment method. A support ticket can preserve the case history when a refund is overdue.

Review the order reference and reason shown at confirmation. If the details need correction, decline and start a new request with the corrected information. Support may ask for supporting material through an existing ticket or follow up using account contact details. Keep evidence of the purchase and use the same ticket while the team is reviewing it.

## Status

A refund can be initiated, processing, or completed. A completed refund has been recorded against the order.


To check progress, use the refund identifier or the order reference from your signed-in account. The timeline depends on the payment method. A customer who has already requested a refund can track the existing record and contact support about delays.
