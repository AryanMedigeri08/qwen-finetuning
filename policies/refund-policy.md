# Refund policy

<!-- intents: check_refund_policy -->

## Eligibility

Request a refund for an eligible delivered order within 7 calendar days of delivery. Include the order number and a short reason.

Keep the reference until the case is resolved. If a refund already exists, check that record before requesting further assistance. An order status and a refund status describe different stages of the purchase.

## Confirmation

Refund initiation requires explicit confirmation for the specified order. Each order has at most one refund record.

If the method or timing is unclear, check the refund timeline policy for the original payment method. A support ticket can preserve the case history when a refund is overdue.

## Status

A refund can be initiated, processing, or completed. A completed refund has been recorded against the order.
