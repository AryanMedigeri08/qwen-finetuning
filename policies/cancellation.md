# Cancellation and fees

<!-- intents: check_cancellation_fee -->

## Before dispatch

Placed or packed orders can be cancelled before dispatch at no fee. Cancellation needs explicit confirmation for that order.

Check the live order status before requesting a cancellation. A confirmation applies only to the order named in the request. If a packed parcel is dispatched while you are deciding, the status at the time of confirmation controls whether the cancellation can proceed.

Before approving, identify the affected purchase by its order reference and review the current status. An item in a separate order has its own cancellation decision, even if the two orders were placed together. If you change your mind at the confirmation prompt, decline the action and the record stays as it was. You can ask again later and check the latest status.

## After dispatch

Shipped or delivered orders cannot be cancelled. After delivery, eligible items can be returned within 7 calendar days.

Review the cancellation receipt and keep the order reference until the refund is settled. For a request involving multiple purchases, identify each purchase and confirm them separately.

When the parcel has already left the fulfilment centre, follow the delivery and returns instructions on the order page. Keep the packaging until you know whether a return is available. If the carrier has marked a parcel delivered but you cannot locate it, open a support ticket so the team can review delivery evidence.

## Refund after cancellation

A confirmed cancellation creates a refund for the order amount. Use the refund reference to check the status.


Check the original payment method and retain the refund identifier. The estimated time depends on the payment method and starts when the refund is initiated. If the refund record already exists, follow that record instead of submitting a duplicate request.
