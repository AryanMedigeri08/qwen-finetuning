# Product reviews

<!-- intents: review -->

## Eligibility

You may submit one review per delivered order item. Open the item from the signed-in purchase history.

General policy applies across the fictional KharidiKart store. Check My Orders for details tied to a particular purchase. The demo displays order information only after selecting the account that owns the order.

Choose the item from the order that contains it. A review may describe how the item performed, its condition at delivery or other relevant product information. Use a support ticket when you need account help, a replacement or a refund.

## Moderation

Reviews usually appear within 2 working days after moderation. Leave order IDs and contact details out of the review.

Use the account portal for a self-service change when the option is available. When contacting support about an existing purchase, keep the order reference handy and describe the outcome you need in a short message.

Moderation checks the review before publication. Check the item's review area after the stated processing window. If the review contains contact information, order identifiers or account details, remove them in your next edit so the public entry is appropriate for other shoppers.

## Edits

You may edit a review for 30 calendar days after posting. Use purchase history to find the existing review.


Open the submitted review from the original account's purchase history to change it. Once the editing window has passed, contact support through the help area if you need to request a correction. Use the support process for an unresolved delivery or payment issue instead of adding private case details to a product review.
