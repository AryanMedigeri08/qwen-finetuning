# Profile and switching accounts

<!-- intents: edit_account switch_account registration_problems -->

## Edit profile

Update your profile from signed-in account settings. Confirm a new email within 30 minutes to apply it.

General policy applies across the fictional KharidiKart store. Check My Orders for details tied to a particular purchase. The demo displays order information only after selecting the account that owns the order.

Open profile settings and review the contact details already on the account before editing them. If verification is required, check the updated inbox for the confirmation message. Return to the settings screen to check that the new information appears after completing verification.

## Switch account

Sign out before signing in to another account. Each session shows only the current account's records.

Use the account portal for a self-service change when the option is available. When contacting support about an existing purchase, keep the order reference handy and describe the outcome you need in a short message.

After switching accounts, verify the account shown before starting an order lookup or a cancellation request. If you use a shared device, close the previous session when you finish. An order can only be handled by the account that placed it, even when several accounts share a device.

## Registration errors

If registration is stuck, check that the email is correct and wait 2 minutes before resending the verification email.


Read any registration error that appears before requesting another email. If an email is already registered, try the recovery route instead of repeating sign-up. When contacting support about repeated errors, describe the stage of registration and the time of your attempt, and keep verification codes private.
